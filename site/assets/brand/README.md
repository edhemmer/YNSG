# Durable print branding

Owner direction, November 1, 2026: all print artwork and signage use only the approved logo, the tagline “We’re Here to Help.” and YourNeighborhoodServiceGuy.com. Do not add telephone numbers, changing prices, email addresses or social handles to print masters.

Current master: ynsg-logo-tagline-website.svg. It embeds the original approved logo and keeps the website as editable text; the logo is raster, so a printer must assess its resolution for the intended physical size. This is a layout master, not a claim of vector logo source or press approval.

Digital contact details remain separate in lib/business-identity.js. Previously issued invoices and other retained financial records are historical evidence and are not regenerated when contact details change.

The cleanout service uses the v3 trailer illustration, whose sign contains the logo, tagline and website without a phone number. The towing assembly and setting were digitally edited; do not describe it as an unedited job photograph.

## Brand font
Headings, buttons and navigation use Rubik (SIL Open Font License, self-hosted in `assets/fonts`). It is the closest free match to the heavy, rounded-corner lettering in the logo. Body copy stays on the system font for speed and readability. Do not add a third typeface.
