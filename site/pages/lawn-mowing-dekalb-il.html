A few helpful answers

How much does lawn mowing cost?

Standard residential lawns up to and including one-third acre start at $50 for mowing, trimming and blowing clippings off finished surfaces. Larger or unusually overgrown lawns need an individual review. See lawn pricing and conditions.

Can I add other yard jobs?

Yes. Add leaf management or garden-bed work to the same request. We’ll review the full list with you; lawn care has separate pricing from hourly home jobs.

What happens after I request a visit?

We review your lawn, access and preferred timing, then confirm the work and price with you. Recurring visits are arranged after confirmation.
