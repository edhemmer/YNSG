How much does lawn mowing cost?

Standard residential lawns up to and including one-third acre start at $50 for mowing, trimming and blowing clippings off finished surfaces. Larger yards and unusually overgrown lawns need an individual review. See lawn pricing and service conditions; any additional work must be agreed on first.

What to include in your mowing request

Tell us the approximate yard size, grass condition and whether gates or other access restrictions affect the work. Mention whether you want a one-time mow or recurring lawn mowing so we can review a suitable plan. A recurring visit is not scheduled until confirmed.

Does mowing include trimming and edging?

Residential lawn care includes mowing, trimming and edging, with clippings blown off driveways and walkways. Leaf gathering or garden-bed work should be listed in your request so the full scope can be reviewed. Lawn care has separate pricing from hourly home jobs.
