Good work starts with clear expectations.

We help with everyday home and yard projects, including smart-home device setup and installation, replacing accessible exterior light fixtures, minor faucet and toilet repairs, lawn care and seasonal cleanup. We’ll review the details with you before confirming the work.

We keep jobs practical and safe, working from the ground or within our five-foot working-height limit. Roofing, gutters, major electrical or plumbing work, structural repairs and projects requiring a trade license or permit are outside our scope.

For outdoor work, we clear residential snow without salt or deicers, pull weeds by hand without herbicides, and pressure wash ground-level concrete using customer-supplied water.
