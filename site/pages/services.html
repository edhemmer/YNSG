Yard & garden

Pulling weeds by hand in garden beds, picking up bulk or bagged mulch, delivering and spreading it, planting small bushes and flowers, garden-bed cleanup, trimming small bushes from the ground, and moving reasonable yard materials. For mulch and larger items, please pay the store or supplier directly before an arranged pickup. We won’t apply herbicides.

Describe your job ↗

A few limits, so there are no surprises.

We don’t work above five feet or on roofs, gutters, second stories or trees. We don’t take on wiring, HVAC repair, structural work, major remodeling, herbicide or other chemical lawn applications, commercial snow work, plowing, snow hauling, salt or deicer.

Smart locks, hardwired lights and plumbing are outside the standard launch menu. If a job needs a licensed trade or falls outside our equipment and experience, we’ll tell you.
