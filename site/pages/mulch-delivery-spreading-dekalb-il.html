A few helpful answers

Can you spread mulch I already have?

Yes. Choose spreading mulch and let us know where the material is stored and which areas need attention. We’ll review the quantity, access and work together.

How do arranged pickup and material payments work?

You pay the store or supplier directly before an arranged pickup. Supplier-prepaid materials are not billed again by us. Pickup labor starts at the supplier; pre-pickup travel requires written approval. Hourly labor has one combined two-hour minimum. See pickup and labor details.

Can I include other yard work?

Absolutely. Add hand weeding, small planting or garden-bed cleanup to the same request. We’ll review the combined work before confirming. No herbicides, tree work, ladders or work above the five-foot limit.
