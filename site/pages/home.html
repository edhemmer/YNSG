Good to know

Questions neighbors ask

How much does it cost?

Home and yard labor is $70 per person per hour, with a two-hour minimum ($140) for the first person, and $45 per person per hour with the Community Rate. We review the work and the price with you before confirming anything. See all rates.

Who can ask for the Community Rate?

Seniors age 70 or older, veterans, single parents and folks with disabilities can ask for $45 per person per hour. You do not need to share private personal details to ask.

What happens after I send a request?

We review the details and reply to confirm the work, price and time. Nothing is booked until we confirm it with you, and no account is needed.

Where do you work?

DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva and St. Charles, plus nearby communities. Check the service area.

Do you use salt when clearing snow?

No. Snow clearing is done with hand shovels or a walk-behind snow blower, without salt or deicer, and we do not offer commercial plowing.

Are disposal fees included in cleanouts?

Disposal fees may apply. We review the scope and any fees with you before confirming a cleanout.
