About Your Neighborhood Service Guy

Owner-operated home and yard services based in the Sycamore and DeKalb area.

“We’re Here to Help.”

Hi, I’m Ed. I started this business to offer practical home and yard services locally. Family experience with aging parents and accessibility needs helped shape the idea. Before my wife and I were together, she was a single mom. I’ve seen how much one extra set of hands can matter when one person is carrying a lot.

Maybe it’s the yard, maybe it’s a few things around the house, or maybe you’re helping a parent arrange work from somewhere else. Tell us what you need. I’ll review the request, say whether I can take it on, and go over the scope, timing and cost before confirming the work.

We’re local to the DeKalb, Sycamore and Cortland area. We especially work with seniors, veterans, single moms and folks with disabilities, and we welcome other neighbors as well.
