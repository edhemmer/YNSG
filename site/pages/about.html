Why we started Your Neighborhood Service Guy.

A local place to bring the jobs around a home and yard that still need doing, even when they don’t call for a contractor.

“We’re Here to Help.”

Hi, I’m Edward, but most people call me Ed. I started this owner-operated service for the ordinary jobs that still need doing, even when they don’t call for a contractor. Family experience with aging parents and accessibility needs helped shape the idea. Before my wife and I were together, she was a single mom. I’ve seen how much one extra set of hands can matter when one person is carrying a lot.

Maybe it’s the yard, maybe it’s a few things around the house, or maybe you’re helping a parent arrange work from somewhere else. Tell us what you need. I’ll review the request, say whether I can take it on, and go over the cost before setting a time.

We’re local to the DeKalb, Sycamore and Cortland area. We especially work with seniors, veterans, single moms and people with disabilities, and we welcome other neighbors as well.
