Mulch and garden-bed projects

We can arrange mulch pickup, delivery and spreading, pull weeds by hand, plant small bushes and flowers, and trim manageable small bushes from the ground.

Preparing for mulch delivery and spreading

Tell us the number and approximate size of your garden beds, whether you want bagged or bulk mulch, and where the material can be unloaded. We can review pickup and spreading as one job, but you pay the supplier directly for the mulch before pickup. We confirm labor and any disposal costs beforehand.

Materials and exclusions

For mulch and larger purchases, customers pay the supplier directly before arranged pickup. Disposal is charged separately where applicable. No herbicides, ladders or work above our five-foot height limit.

What to include for mulch spreading

Describe the garden beds, approximate area and access to the work. Tell us whether mulch is already at your home or needs arranged pickup. For pickup, include the supplier and whether the material is bulk or bagged so we can review the plan.

Who pays for mulch and pickup?

You pay the supplier directly before an arranged pickup. Supplier-prepaid mulch is not billed again by us. Pickup labor starts at the supplier; pre-pickup travel requires written approval. One combined two-hour minimum applies to hourly labor. Review the mulch pickup and labor details before requesting the work.
