A few helpful answers

Who pays for mulch and pickup?

You pay the supplier directly before an arranged pickup. Supplier-prepaid mulch is not billed again by us. Pickup labor starts at the supplier; pre-pickup travel requires written approval. Hourly labor has one combined two-hour minimum. See material pickup and labor details.

What outdoor work fits this service?

Hand weeding, mulch spreading, garden-bed cleanup, small planting jobs and manageable small-bush trimming from the ground. We review materials and any disposal costs before the work. Herbicides, ladders and work above the five-foot limit are outside this service.
