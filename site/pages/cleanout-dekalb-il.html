A few helpful answers

What does a cleanout cost?

Labor is charged at the applicable hourly rate: $60 per hour, or $45 per hour for qualifying Community Rate neighbors, with one combined two-hour minimum. Disposal fees may apply. Any disposal charges are reviewed before proceeding and paid to the disposal facility by YNSG. See rates and Community Rate details.

What can you take?

Manageable household clutter and items that can be handled safely by one person. Hazardous materials, large appliances and excessively heavy items are excluded. We review suitability, access and the disposal plan with you first.

Can I combine a cleanout with another job?

Yes. Add other services to the same request, and we’ll review the work and timing together. A request does not guarantee an appointment or quote.
