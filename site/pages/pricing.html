Who can ask for the Community Rate?

Seniors age 70 or older, veterans, single moms, and people with disabilities. We serve everyone; this rate is one way we can especially help those neighbors. You won’t need to share a diagnosis, income information or a hardship story.

What about lawn, snow and materials?

Standard residential lawns up to and including one-third acre start at $50. Larger yards are reviewed and quoted. Snow clearing is priced separately. The Community Rate applies to approved hourly home and yard labor; it does not automatically discount lawn care, snow, materials, parts or disposal.

Materials, parts and approved disposal fees are separate. If we need to buy something, we’ll ask before spending and charge the documented actual cost without an automatic markup.
