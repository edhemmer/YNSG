Who can ask for the Community Rate?

Seniors age 70 or older, veterans, single moms, and folks with disabilities. We serve everyone; this rate is one way we can especially help those neighbors. You don’t need to share private personal details to ask about the Community Rate.

What about lawn, snow and materials?

Standard residential lawns up to and including one-third acre start at $50. Larger yards are reviewed and quoted. Snow clearing and concrete pressure washing are priced separately. The Community Rate applies to approved hourly home and yard labor; it does not automatically discount lawn care, snow, concrete pressure washing, materials, parts or disposal.

For mulch and larger items, you place and pay for the order directly with the store or supplier before an arranged pickup. If we agree to pick it up, hourly labor starts when pickup begins at the supplier and includes loading, delivery, unloading and any agreed spreading or installation. The two-hour minimum applies to that combined time, not a separate minimum for pickup. For smaller approved purchases, materials, parts and disposal are separate at documented actual cost without an automatic markup. We’ll ask before spending.
