How estimates and billing work

Hourly work has a two-hour minimum per visit, which we confirm in your estimate before scheduling. When multiple tasks can be completed on one visit, we estimate the combined work rather than automatically charging separate minimums. Additional team members, if needed, are discussed as part of the agreed estimate and cap. Some services—including lawn care and seasonal work—are quoted per job.

We’ll agree on a scope, estimated cost and any not-to-exceed cap before work begins. If conditions change or additional work is needed, we’ll explain the options and request approval before exceeding the agreed limit.

Farm hand services

Farm hand work has a four-hour minimum and is quoted for the requested tasks, dates and location before scheduling.

Property owners and managers

We also provide individually quoted support for rental-property upkeep, turnover tasks, seasonal grounds work and approved cleanouts. Ask about recurring arrangements for multiple properties.

Materials, pickups and disposal

Materials, parts, hauling and disposal may involve additional approved charges. For arranged mulch or larger-item pickups, the customer generally orders and pays the supplier directly. We’ll explain labor, pickup time and any additional charges as part of the quote.
