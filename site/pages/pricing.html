Residential service estimates

We review the work, any access or equipment needs, and the expected time before confirming your price. Minimum charges or fixed-price services will be explained in your individual estimate.

Helping our neighbors

Community Rate

Seniors age 70 or older, veterans, single moms, and folks with disabilities can ask about our Community Rate for eligible hourly work. No proof or private personal explanation is needed.

Landlords and property managers

We provide individual estimates for approved rental-property work, maintenance visits, grounds care and cleanouts. If you have multiple properties or recurring needs, tell us so we can discuss a suitable service arrangement.

Materials and additional costs

Some work may involve materials, parts, delivery, hauling or disposal. We’ll explain any applicable charges and request approval before incurring them. For mulch and larger items, customers typically purchase directly from the supplier before an agreed pickup.

How to get an estimate

Send us your service request and any useful details. We’ll confirm the approved scope, price, and appointment with you before work begins. Submitting a request does not obligate you to book.
