How we work with you

  1. Send the list and photos. Use the request form and include the property address, access details and who approves the work.
  2. Approve an estimate and a not-to-exceed cap. See typical times and costs in our job time guide.
  3. We do the work on the agreed day. If anything will run past the cap, we stop and ask first.
  4. You get before-and-after photos and an itemized invoice.

Standard rate $70 per person per hour, first-person two-hour minimum. We don’t perform work that requires a licensed trade, such as plumbing, electrical, HVAC or roofing; if a job needs one, we’ll tell you.
