Listing & rental help

What we take off your list

For listings

  • Pre-listing punch lists: door hardware, sticky doors, shelving, small drywall patches, curtain rods and blinds.
  • Curb appeal: mowing, leaf management, weed pulling, mulch and bush trimming before photos and showings.
  • Clear the clutter: garage, basement and storage-area cleanouts so rooms show their size.

For rentals

  • Turnovers: cleanouts of items left behind, small repairs and a yard reset between tenants, including the busy May and August student move-outs around NIU.
  • Seasonal upkeep: leaves in the fall, snow clearing in winter, mulch and cleanup in spring.
  • One-off fixes: the small items tenants report that don’t need a licensed trade.

Inspection report fixes

Send us the inspection report and the items the buyer and seller agreed on. Let us take a look and see how we can help. Many reports list everyday items we can take care of quickly, often before closing:

  • Doors and hardware: doors that stick or won’t latch, loose knobs and hinges, missing door stops, cabinet doors and drawer slides.
  • Gaps and seals: caulk at tubs, showers, sinks and window trim, small grout repairs, worn door weatherstripping and sweeps.
  • Walls and screens: small drywall patches and nail pops, torn window and door screens, loose towel bars.
  • Around the house: chirping smoke alarm batteries, clearing stored items away from the furnace and water heater, and trimming shrubs back from the siding.

Items that need a licensed plumber, electrician, HVAC technician or roofer, or a licensed contractor’s repair receipt, we’ll point out right away so you can line up the right trade. You get before-and-after photos and an itemized invoice for the work we did to share with your client.

How we work with you

  1. Send the list and photos. Use the request form and include the property address, access details and who approves the work.
  2. Approve an estimate and a not-to-exceed cap. We confirm the price for your approved work order before starting. See how our estimates work.
  3. We do the work on the agreed day. If anything will run past the cap, we stop and ask first.
  4. You get before-and-after photos and an itemized invoice.

We charge a fair hourly rate, with the scope, visit minimum and any additional costs confirmed in each estimate. We don’t perform work that requires a licensed trade, such as plumbing, electrical, HVAC or roofing; if a job needs one, we’ll tell you.
