Season by season

Home and yard calendar for the DeKalb area

Spring

  • Clear winter debris and fallen branches from the lawn and beds.
  • Edge beds and refresh mulch once the ground has thawed.
  • Wash winter salt and grime off driveways, walks and patios.
  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and replace weak batteries.

We can help with: yard cleanup, mulch pickup and spreading, concrete washing, first mowing of the season.

Summer

  • Keep the lawn on a steady mowing schedule through the growing season.
  • Pull weeds before they set seed and keep mulch pulled back from siding and foundation.
  • Walk the house for small items that were put off: sticking doors, loose hardware, torn screens.

We can help with: lawn mowing and trimming, garden-bed care, small home jobs.

Fall

  • Stay ahead of falling leaves so they don’t mat down the lawn.
  • Disconnect garden hoses before the first hard freeze.
  • Schedule your annual furnace check with a licensed heating and cooling company before the cold sets in.
  • Put away patio furniture, and clear paths and entries.

We can help with: leaf management, final cleanup, cleanouts of garages and storage areas before winter.

Winter

  • Keep walks, steps and entries clear and treated so they’re safe to use.
  • Keep stored items away from the furnace and water heater.
  • Make a plan now for who clears the snow when you can’t.

We can help with: residential snow clearing for driveways, walks and entries, and indoor small jobs while the weather keeps you inside.

Which pro do I call?

Part of doing right by a neighbor is knowing what’s ours to do and what isn’t. Here’s how we sort it.

Call us

  • Small home repairs and adjustments, assembly and wall-mounted items.
  • Lawn, leaf, mulch, weed and garden-bed work.
  • Cleanouts and hauling of manageable household items.
  • Accessibility help, such as grab bars, for you or a loved one.
  • Concrete washing and residential snow clearing.

Call a licensed pro

  • Plumbing leaks, water heaters and drain problems.
  • Electrical wiring, panels, outlets that spark or run hot.
  • Furnace, air conditioning and other heating and cooling work.
  • Roofing, gutters and anything high up.
  • Large tree removal and work that needs a permit.

If we visit and find one of these, we’ll tell you right away so you can line up the right trade.

Call 911 first

If you smell gas, leave the home right away and call 911 from outside, then call your gas utility. Sparks, smoke or fire, or a carbon monoxide alarm sounding with symptoms, are emergencies too. We’re not an emergency service, so please don’t wait for us.

Before you dig

Planting, fences and digging

Illinois law asks you to have underground utility lines marked before most digging. Contact JULIE by calling 811, or visit illinois1call.com, a few working days ahead. It’s free, and it protects you and your crew.

Choosing someone to do the work

Whether it’s us or anyone else, a good contractor is easy to spot when you know what to look for.

Before you hire

  • Ask neighbors and friends who they’ve used and would call again.
  • Look up the business: a real local address, a working phone number and a way to reach them after the job.
  • Read recent reviews, and look for how they handled problems, not just the stars.
  • Ask who will actually be doing the work.
  • Be careful with anyone who shows up unannounced, pressures you to decide today, or finds “extra” problems you didn’t ask about.

The estimate and agreement

  • Get the scope, price and timing in writing before work starts. For larger home repair and remodeling jobs, Illinois law asks for a written contract.
  • For bigger jobs, compare two or three written estimates, and compare what’s included, not just the price.
  • Ask what happens if the job turns out bigger than expected, and who approves any change in writing.
  • For trades that require a license, ask to see it.
  • Ask about the right to cancel, and how to reach them with questions.

During and after the job

  • Be cautious of anyone asking for most of the payment up front, or for cash only.
  • Hold the final payment until you’ve walked through the finished work.
  • Keep your estimate, invoices and before-and-after photos together.
  • Ask whether the work comes with any guarantee, and get that in writing too.
  • The Illinois Attorney General’s office offers free consumer help if something goes wrong.

Getting ready for your visit

Before we arrive

  • Send photos with your request so we can plan tools and time.
  • Tell us about gates, pets and where to park.
  • Clear the work area where you can, and let us know about anything fragile.

While we’re there

  • We confirm the scope with you and stay within the estimate and cap you approved.
  • If something new comes up, we stop and ask before going further.

When we’re done

  • You get a walkthrough of the finished work.
  • You get an itemized invoice, with photos of finished work when it helps.
