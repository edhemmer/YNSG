Extra farm help when you need it

We can review practical, agreed tasks for spring planting preparation, fall harvest support, seasonal cleanup and day-to-day farm work. You provide the work plan and site details; we confirm what one person can safely handle.

  • Spring planting support and general preparation
  • Fall harvest support and non-specialized ground tasks

  • Other general farm labor by agreement

Clear day rate

$300 for one day of farm help, up to 10 hours in that day. The rate is not a promise of 10 continuous working hours; breaks and applicable requirements are respected. This is a separate farm labor rate, not the home and yard hourly rate. Additional hours, consecutive days, supplies and travel arrangements are discussed and agreed on before work begins.

Before we confirm

What should I include in the request?

Share the farm address, the dates you need help, the expected start and finish times, the type of work and any special access or safety considerations. Use the notes box to describe your farm tasks. Choose the option for YNSG to arrange the time rather than a standard short appointment slot.

Can you operate equipment?

Machine operation, hazardous or licensed work and specialized agricultural tasks are not automatically included. We review the duties, training, insurance and safety requirements before accepting any job.

Can I request more than one day?

Yes. Describe the number of days and the work you need. We will review availability and confirm the arrangement. This service offers one extra farm hand, not a staffed crew.
