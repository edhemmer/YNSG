Before we confirm

What should I include in the request?

Share the farm address, the dates you need help, the expected start and finish times, the type of work and any special access or safety considerations. Use the notes box to describe your farm tasks. Choose the option for YNSG to arrange the time rather than a standard short appointment slot.

Can you operate equipment?

Machine operation, hazardous or licensed work and specialized agricultural tasks are not automatically included. We review the duties, training, insurance and safety requirements before accepting any job.

Can I request more than one day?

Yes. Describe the number of days and the work you need. We will review availability and confirm the arrangement. This service offers one extra farm hand, not a staffed crew.

Serving farms in DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland and nearby communities, subject to availability and safe scope. Requests from other nearby towns are welcome for review.
