Website terms

Using this website

These terms explain what a request through the site means.

Your Neighborhood Service Guy, LLC is the business operating this website and providing the services described here.

Requests are inquiries

Sending a form or leaving a message asks us to review a possible job. It does not guarantee availability, accept a job, confirm an appointment or establish a final price. If you choose an available time, it may be held temporarily while your request is submitted and reviewed. A temporary hold is not a confirmed appointment. We review the work, location and conditions before agreeing to service.

Service scope and pricing

The service descriptions and published rates explain our general offer. The details of an individual job, including any materials or disposal, are discussed and confirmed before scheduling. Work outside our scope may be declined or referred to an appropriate licensed trade.

Information you provide

Please provide enough accurate contact and job details for us to respond. Do not submit another person’s sensitive information or payment card details. See our Privacy Notice for how request details are handled.

Questions

Call or text 815-680-1775. Accepted work is documented in a job-specific service agreement.
