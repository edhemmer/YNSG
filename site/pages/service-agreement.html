Service agreement

Clear on the work before we begin.

Here’s how we agree on a job, handle changes and address concerns.

These service terms describe our general approach. A job is accepted only after we confirm its written scope, pricing and appointment with you. Sending a website request does not accept a contract. Keep the job-specific agreement, approvals and invoice for your records.

The written job details

The agreement identifies the customer or authorized representative, the service provider’s legal name and business address, the property, agreed tasks, exclusions, materials, expected timing, price or labor rate, minimum, payment schedule and any applicable cancellation notice. Estimates describe anticipated work; a fixed price applies only when expressly agreed. Any limit on spending or hours belongs in the written scope.

Hourly home and yard labor is $60 per hour, with a two-hour minimum of $120. The Community Rate is $45 per hour, with a two-hour minimum of $90, for approved hourly labor. Lawn care, snow clearing and concrete pressure washing are priced separately. Materials, parts and disposal are separate authorized costs. An estimate is not a promise that hidden conditions will not affect the work.

Licensed work and service limits

Any work requiring a trade license, permit or code-regulated installation must be performed by an appropriately licensed contractor. We do not accept that work as ordinary handyman service. Before any separate arrangement, the written scope must identify the responsible contractor, required credentials and permits, pricing and responsibility for inspections. A referral alone does not mean we have hired that contractor or guaranteed their availability or work.

Our published services and limits still apply. We may decline work outside our lawful scope, equipment, experience or safe working conditions. Website images illustrate the neighbors we serve; they do not expand our service limits or promise an individual result.

Changes and unexpected conditions

Extra work, substitutions and additional charges require your written approval before proceeding. Email or an agreed written message can document a change. If we find unsafe access, concealed damage, unsuitable materials or work beyond the approved scope, we may stop, document the issue and discuss options. We will not treat your silence as approval. A pause does not authorize additional work or an unapproved charge.

Access, property and safety

Please arrange lawful permission to work, accessible entry, a clear work area and secure pets. Tell us about known hazards, fragile surfaces and relevant restrictions without sending private medical details. Concrete pressure washing requires accessible customer-supplied outdoor water. We may postpone or stop if weather, access or site conditions make the agreed work unsafe. Snow clearing does not guarantee an ice-free or slip-free surface; salt and deicer are outside our services.

Each party remains responsible for its own acts and omissions under applicable law. Disclosing a hazard does not transfer our responsibility to you, and these terms do not release us from liability the law does not allow us to exclude.

Mulch and larger-item pickup

You place and pay for mulch and larger-item orders directly with the supplier before an agreed pickup. We do not advance that purchase price. The written job details identify the items, supplier, safe handling limits and agreed delivery or application.

Hourly labor starts when pickup begins at the supplier and includes loading, time there, delivery, unloading and agreed application. The two-hour minimum applies once to that combined work, not separately to pickup and on-site work. Travel to the supplier before pickup is excluded unless separately agreed in writing. An unavailable order, extra trip or changed quantity requires discussion and approval before extra charges. Smaller approved purchases and disposal are charged at documented actual cost without an automatic markup.

Timing, cancellation and recurring visits

Appointment windows and route estimates can change because of weather, traffic, prior work or unsafe conditions. They are not guaranteed arrival or completion times. Contact us promptly to cancel or reschedule. Any cancellation or access-related charge must be expressly agreed in writing beforehand and remain lawful; this page does not create a cancellation fee.

Either party may end future service by notifying the other, subject to the job-specific agreement and applicable law. Recurring visits require an agreed schedule and pricing; a weekly request alone creates no subscription, automatic renewal or permission to charge future visits. Earned, authorized charges remain payable unless the law provides otherwise. Unused advance payments and required refunds are handled according to the agreement and applicable law.

Payment, completion and concerns

The job agreement states when payment is due and the available methods. Any lawful payment fee must be disclosed and approved before payment. Do not send card details through our request form. Review the itemized invoice and let us know about an error or work concern so we can assess it. Contacting us does not limit your right to pursue a lawful remedy or dispute a charge.

Completed, authorized work is generally nonrefundable, except where a refund is required by law or agreed to resolve a concern. We do not promise a particular outcome, savings, completion speed, accessibility certification or blanket satisfaction guarantee. Any express warranty must be written in the job agreement; legally required protections still apply.

Your consumer rights

For work covered by Illinois home-repair law, the required written agreement, consumer-rights brochure and acknowledgment must be supplied as applicable. Covered work over $1,000 requires a signed written contract or work order with costs detailed before work begins. This webpage is not a substitute for those documents.

Certain sales made at a home or another covered location carry cancellation rights, including a three-business-day right where applicable. The required notices and forms belong with the actual transaction. Online requests do not determine whether a later agreement qualifies. Nothing here waives statutory cancellation, refund, warranty or other consumer rights, requires binding arbitration, or waives a jury trial. Illinois law applies subject to any mandatory protections that also govern the job.
