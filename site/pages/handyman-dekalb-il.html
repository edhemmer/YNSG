A few helpful answers

What does a small handyman visit cost?

Hourly home labor is $60 per hour with one combined two-hour minimum ($120). Eligible neighbors can ask about the $45 per hour Community Rate with a two-hour minimum ($90). Materials are separate. See rates and Community Rate details.

What kinds of jobs fit?

Furniture assembly, household product setup, shelving, lightweight hanging, door hardware, customer-supplied smart lock installation on compatible existing doors, household hardware adjustments and minor drywall patches are practical examples. For locks, tell us the model and existing door hardware. We review compatibility first; no hardwiring, security-system integration, emergency lockouts or work requiring a license or permit. Describe anything unusual so we can review the tools, materials and time.

Good to know before we visit

Work stays at ground level or within our five-foot working-height limit. Roofing, gutters, major plumbing or electrical work, structural repairs, and work requiring a trade license or permit are outside this service.
