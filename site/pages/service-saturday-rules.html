Official Rules

Plain-English rules for our free Service Saturday giveaway. Back to Service Saturday

No purchase necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is needed to enter or win. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. The prize is home and yard service labor only. It has no cash value and can’t be exchanged for money. Void where prohibited.

Sponsor

Your Neighborhood Service Guy LLC, an Illinois limited liability company, 298 Quinlan Ave, DeKalb, IL 60115 (“we,” “us”). Contact: info@yourneighborhoodserviceguy.com or 815-680-1775.

Who can enter

Legal residents of Illinois who are 18 or older. Our owner and anyone who works for us, and members of their immediate families and households, cannot enter or win. The work location must be a home in or near our service area (DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva and St. Charles).

How to enter

Complete the entry form at yourneighborhoodserviceguy.com/service-saturday between 12:00 AM Central time on {{SS_START_LONG}} and 11:59 PM Central time on {{SS_DEADLINE_LONG}}. You can enter for your own home or nominate someone else's home. If you can't use the form, call or email us with the same information and we'll enter you. Limit one entry per person and per email address. Entries that are incomplete, false, automated or sent after the deadline are not eligible.

Nominations

If you nominate someone, please tell them. If a nomination wins, the prize is for the nominated home. The nominated person (and the property owner, if they rent) must agree to the help before we schedule anything. If they decline or we can't reach them, we'll draw another entry.

Before the visit

Before we schedule the work, the winner (and the property owner, if different) will confirm the job list with us and sign a short acceptance form agreeing to these rules, the work to be done and a release for ordinary risks of the work. If they choose not to sign, we may draw another entry.

Winner selection

We will choose one winner by random drawing from all eligible entries on or about {{SS_DRAWING_LONG}}. The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received. We'll contact the winner by email or phone. If the winner doesn't respond within 3 days, or isn't eligible, we may draw another entry.

The prize

Up to {{SS_HOURS}} hours of labor by one Your Neighborhood Service Guy worker, used in one visit on {{SS_SERVICE_LONG}} between 8 AM and 5 PM. If weather or safety requires, we'll agree on another date with the winner for that single visit. Approximate retail value: ${{SS_VALUE}}.

  • Labor only. Materials, parts, supplies, equipment rental and disposal or dump fees are not included. Work that needs materials can be done only if the winner already has them on hand.
  • Work is limited to the everyday home and yard services we offer, at ground level. We don't do roof or gutter work, tree work, electrical, plumbing or other licensed trades, or anything involving hazardous materials.
  • We may decline any task we decide is unsafe or outside our services, and suggest a different task instead.
  • The hours must be used in one visit. They can't be split across visits, saved for later, transferred to another person or home, or exchanged for money. Unused hours don't carry over.
  • The winner is responsible for any taxes on the prize.

We'd love to share the story, but we'll only use the winner's name, town or photos with their permission. We never show house numbers or anyone's face without consent.
