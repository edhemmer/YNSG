Accessibility

We want this site to be easy to use.

You can use the form, call or text us—whichever works best for you.

Using the website

The site is designed for keyboard navigation, readable text, clear form labels and reduced motion settings. You can enlarge text in your browser. The service menu can be opened with a keyboard, and movement is reduced when your device asks for that.

If something gets in your way

Call or text 770-630-2094 or Email us. Tell us which page or step is difficult and how you’d prefer us to respond. We can take the request by phone or text.

We welcome feedback and will work to fix access problems we can identify. Our goal is WCAG 2.2 AA; that is a target, not a claim of certification.
