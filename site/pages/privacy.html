Privacy Notice

Here’s what happens when you contact us through this website.

What we receive

The request form asks for a name, phone number, email, service address, city, service choice and a short description. Timing preference and Community Rate inquiry are optional. Please don’t send payment card details, a diagnosis, door codes or other sensitive information in the form.

How we use it

We use the information to review your request, respond, discuss scope and pricing, and arrange service if we both agree to proceed. A submission does not create an account. Your request is saved in our business service system. We email you a receipt and notify the business so we can review it. Website hosting and email delivery also create routine technical logs. We use keyed digests of network addresses and email addresses to limit repeated automated requests; this adds no extra step to your form.

Unfinished requests

Your unfinished request is kept in this browser tab for up to 24 hours so you can return after a refresh. Nothing is sent until you press Send service request. Use Clear saved details to remove an unfinished draft, or close the tab. If a send still needs confirmation, retry the same request before starting another. Details clear after a confirmed successful send.

Sharing and retention

We use service providers to host the site, store service records and deliver messages. We do not sell the information you submit. We keep request correspondence as needed to respond, provide service, maintain business records and resolve questions.

Contact

To ask about your request information or request a correction or deletion where feasible, call or text 770-630-2094 or Email us.
