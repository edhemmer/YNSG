Privacy Notice

How we handle your details when you use the website or arrange service.

Information we receive

Your Neighborhood Service Guy receives the name, phone number, email, service address, city, selected jobs and notes you submit. Timing preferences and a Community Rate inquiry are optional. No proof, diagnosis or personal explanation is needed for the Community Rate. Please do not send medical records, payment-card details, passwords or entry codes through the request form.

When you arrange service, our records may also include agreed appointments, job notes, quotes, invoices, payment status and correspondence. Website and service providers process technical information such as network address, request time, browser information and operational logs. Our anti-abuse system uses keyed digests of network and email addresses to limit repeated automated requests; digests are not a claim that all technical data is anonymous.

What happens before you send

Unfinished form details are saved in this browser tab for up to 24 hours to support recovery after a refresh, where browser storage is available. Clear saved details removes the saved draft. Details clear after a confirmed successful send; shared devices and browser session restoration may retain a tab, so clear the draft before leaving a shared device.

Opening the calendar checks available times. Selecting a time sends the chosen time and technical identifiers to create a temporary hold. These checks do not send your full contact-and-job form as a service request. Pressing Send service request sends that form to our service system. A pending send may already have reached us; retry the same request to check it rather than assuming nothing was received.

How we use your information

We use it to review requests, communicate, arrange and perform approved service, plan routes, maintain job and billing records, prevent misuse and address disputes or legal obligations. Sending a request does not create an account or subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

The system saves the request and queues a receipt and business notification. Appointment updates, reminders and follow-up messages may be generated from service records. Delivery depends on the service providers and your contact details; email, text and phone are not guaranteed instant channels. If you use a sign-in portal, session information is used to authenticate and protect access.

Service providers and sharing

We use providers for hosting, database storage, authentication, email, calendar and route services. Our current website and CRM use Vercel, Supabase and Google services. Calendar integration can process appointment details; route estimation sends service addresses and departure times to Google. Opening a Maps link connects you to Google under its own policies. We share only information relevant to the provider’s function, and provider systems may process data outside your state or country.

If you approve a job involving a separate contractor or supplier, relevant contact, location and job details may be shared to arrange that work. We may also disclose records when required by law or reasonably needed to protect rights, safety or address misuse. We do not sell the information you submit. Marketing use of your identifiable photo or testimonial requires separate permission; website illustrations are not actual customer records.

Payments, browser storage and links

Do not enter card details into a service request. If an approved job offers an external payment link, payment information is handled by that payment provider under its own policy. Our service records can include invoice amounts and payment status.

This site uses tab storage for unfinished requests and operational identifiers for appointment holds. A portal can use authentication cookies. Our current public request site does not include advertising trackers. Third-party sites opened from links have their own storage and privacy practices; this notice does not control them.

Retention and security

We keep records as needed for requests, service, accounting, legal requirements and resolving disputes; not every record has the same retention period. Deletion may be limited by these obligations and copies in routine backups. Restricted access and technical safeguards help protect records, but no website, email system or storage method can promise absolute security. If a qualifying security incident occurs, legally required notifications apply.

Your choices and contacting us

You can contact us by phone or email instead of using the form. Ask to access, correct or delete your information, or stop optional promotional messages, at edhemmer@gmail.com or 770-630-2094. We may need to verify the request and retain records required by law. Service-related notices may still be needed for an active job.

The service-request site is intended for adults arranging property services, not for children under 13 to submit their own information. Contact us if a child has submitted personal information. Future material changes to this notice will be dated here and any additional notice required by law will be provided.
