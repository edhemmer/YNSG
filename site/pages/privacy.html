Privacy Notice

Here’s what happens when you contact us through this website.

What we receive

The request form asks for a name, phone number, email, service address, city, service choice and a short description. Timing preference and Community Rate inquiry are optional. Please don’t send payment card details, a diagnosis, door codes or other sensitive information in the form.

How we use it

We use the information to review your request, respond, discuss scope and pricing, and arrange service if we both agree to proceed. A submission does not create an account. The request is emailed to our business inbox at edhemmer@gmail.com through our email delivery provider, Resend. Website hosting and delivery also create routine technical logs.

Sharing and retention

We use service providers to host the site and deliver messages. We do not sell the information you submit. We keep request correspondence as needed to respond, provide service, maintain business records and resolve questions. A future customer account or CRM will have its own published details when those features are available.

Contact

To ask about your request information or request a correction or deletion where feasible, call or text 770-630-2094 or email edhemmer@gmail.com.
