Where we may fit

Builders & developers

Final punch lists, small drywall patches, door hardware, light assembly, garage cleanup and move-in preparation. We work to an agreed scope and fit into your site requirements.

Landlords & property managers

Between-tenant cleanouts, small permitted-scope fixes, lawn and leaf work, ground-level exterior upkeep and property-specific work orders.

Farm managers & operators

Seasonal extra-hand support, farmstead cleanup, material handling and light grounds work within an agreed safe scope. No equipment operation is assumed.

Community & referral organizations

A local contact for neighbors who need help around the home or yard. We offer a Community Rate for eligible neighbors; referrals are never a promise of free service or organizational endorsement.

How we work

We review each request personally, agree on a practical scope and price before work starts, and respect your site's access and safety requirements. We don't take on licensed electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structural, roof or gutter work, elevated tasks, or commercial snow plowing. No promises of emergency or 24-hour service.

We're a locally owned small business, not a large maintenance crew. For a good fit, we can start with a single job and see whether a repeat relationship makes sense.
