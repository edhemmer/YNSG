Who we work with

One dependable call for the small jobs

Landlords & property managers

Rental turnovers between tenants: cleanouts of items left behind, wall patches, touch-ups, door and cabinet hardware, caulk and a yard reset so the unit shows well. Overflow help when your regular crew is booked. See listing prep and inspection report fixes.

Commercial & mixed-use building owners

Storefronts with apartments upstairs, small strip buildings and single-tenant buildings. Vacant-space cleanouts and punch lists so a space is ready to show, plus ground-level upkeep of shared areas and beds.

Churches & places of worship

Older buildings always have a list: doors and closers, locks, trim, shelving, wall patches, grab bars and accessibility help for members and guests, grounds cleanup, and setup before events and services. We’re glad to walk the building with your trustees or facilities team.

Offices & small businesses

Shelving and furniture assembly, door and hardware fixes, wall patching and tidy beds out front, done with as little disruption to your day as we can manage.

Builders & developers

An extra hand before a walkthrough or move-in: small drywall patches, door hardware adjustments, light assembly and garage cleanup. We review site access and requirements before agreeing to a job.

Farms & community organizations

Occasional extra-hand help and light farmstead upkeep, reviewed for safe duties first. Explore our farm-hand services. Helping a senior, veteran or family find home help? We welcome introductions and offer a Community Rate for eligible neighbors.

How we work with your business

  1. Free walkthrough. We look at the property with you and go over the list.
  2. A clear written estimate. You see what’s included before any work starts.
  3. Nothing extra without your OK. If something new comes up, we check with you first.
  4. The work on the agreed day. We confirm the day and come ready to work.
  5. An itemized invoice for each property, with photos of the finished work. A W-9 is available on request.

We reply within one business day, Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. See how our estimates work.

Popular for business accounts

  • Fix-It Morning: a morning set aside to work through your punch list.
  • Turnover help: a vacant unit or space made ready to show.
  • Standing work list: scheduled monthly or quarterly visits for ongoing upkeep.

What we don’t do

We focus on smaller, ground-level jobs. Licensed plumbing, electrical and HVAC work, permit work, roofing, gutter work, anything above five feet, parking lots and commercial snow plowing are outside our scope. If a job needs one of those trades, we’ll tell you right away.

Based in DeKalb, we work most often in DeKalb, Sycamore and Cortland, and welcome inquiries from Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva, St. Charles and nearby communities.
