Home Service Tech in DeKalb: small home jobs, done right.

What a typical visit looks like

You arrive in the window we promised the customer, introduce yourself and walk through the list together. Then you get to work: assemble a bed frame, hang four pictures and a mirror, swap a deadbolt, put up two shelves. You clean up, walk the customer through what you did, and snap before-and-after photos for their visit report. Most visits run two to four hours.

The work

  • Furniture assembly and household setup
  • Door hardware: knobs, deadbolts, hinges, stops
  • Shelving, curtain rods, blinds, pictures and mirrors
  • Small drywall patches
  • Home punch lists for realtors and landlords

We don’t do work that needs a licensed trade, like plumbing, electrical, HVAC or roofing. Every job has an agreed scope, so you never have to guess or upsell.

You’ll do well here if you

  • Show up when you say you will, every time
  • Have hands-on experience with tools and home projects, paid or not
  • Treat a customer’s home with care and leave it cleaner than you found it
  • Explain what you did in plain words, and ask before doing anything extra
  • Can get yourself to jobs between DeKalb and St. Charles

Retired from the trades and want to stay sharp a few days a week? You’re exactly who our customers love to see.

Where you’ll work

Homes in DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva, St. Charles and nearby communities.

Requirements

Age 18 or older and applicable work authorization. Veterans are encouraged to apply. All qualified applicants are welcome.

How to apply

The application takes about 5 minutes: contact information, the skills you’re strongest in, your experience and availability. A PDF resume up to 1 MB is optional, and no account is needed. Hours grow with customer demand; we’ll be straight with you about what’s available. Please keep ID documents and other sensitive records out of your application. Need an accommodation?
