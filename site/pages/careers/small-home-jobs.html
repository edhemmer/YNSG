The work you could help with

Agreed small home jobs may include furniture assembly, household hardware, organizing, shelving and minor drywall patches within our service limits. Each assignment is reviewed for scope, access, equipment and safe handling. Tell us which tasks you know well and where you would need guidance.

What makes a good fit

  • Relevant hands-on experience and care for a customer’s home
  • Dependability, safe work habits and clear communication
  • Comfort following the agreed scope and asking before taking on extra work
  • Ability to arrange reliable travel to local jobs
  • Age 18 or older and applicable work authorization

Veterans are encouraged to apply. All qualified applicants are welcome. We review job-related experience and availability individually.

Local work, discussed honestly

Our home service area includes DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva, St. Charles and neighboring communities. Opportunities depend on customer demand, relevant skills and availability. Scheduling, responsibilities and terms are discussed before any work arrangement. There are no guaranteed hours, assignments or start dates.

How to apply

Complete the short application with your contact information, relevant skills, work experience and availability. A PDF resume up to 1 MB is optional. No account is required. Current application availability is shown before you submit.

Need an accommodation or another way to apply? Call 815-680-1775. Please do not submit ID documents or other sensitive personal records with your application.
