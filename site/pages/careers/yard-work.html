Seasonal work, useful skills

Agreed tasks may include lawn and garden upkeep, leaf management, mulch handling, residential snow clearing and manageable household cleanouts. Work changes with the season and customer requests. We review the task, conditions and safe handling before assigning work.

What we look for

  • Relevant lawn, garden, seasonal cleanup or other practical experience
  • Dependability and respect for customers and their property
  • Safe use of equipment you are qualified and authorized to operate
  • Clear communication about progress, conditions and questions
  • Ability to arrange reliable travel to local jobs
  • Age 18 or older and applicable work authorization

Veterans are encouraged to apply. All qualified applicants are welcome. Share the skills you already have and the tasks you would like to learn.

Serving neighbors close to home

Our home and yard service area includes DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva, St. Charles and neighboring communities. Outdoor scheduling depends on weather, safe conditions, customer demand and availability. Responsibilities, scheduling and terms are discussed directly before any work arrangement. Applying does not guarantee hours, assignments or a start date.

A simple application

Share your contact information, relevant experience, skills and availability. You can describe your work history instead of uploading a resume. An optional PDF resume may be up to 1 MB. No account is needed, and the application page shows whether intake is currently open.

Need an accommodation or help applying? Call 815-680-1775. Keep ID documents and other sensitive personal records out of your application.
