Crew Member · DeKalb to St. Charles

Crew Member in DeKalb: yard and seasonal work outdoors.

Like being outside, staying active and finishing the day with something to show for it? Join the crew that keeps our neighbors’ yards and driveways in shape all year.

  • Competitive hourly pay
  • Flexible, part-time schedule
  • Great for students and early risers
Apply for Crew Member ↗

What the work looks like through the year

  • Fall: leaf management and garden-bed cleanup
  • Winter: residential snow clearing for driveways, walks and entries, often early mornings after a storm
  • Spring: mulch pickup and spreading, weeding, planting and bush trimming
  • Summer: mowing, trimming and edging
  • Any time: garage, basement and storage cleanouts

A typical job runs two to five hours, often with a second crew member. Every job has an agreed scope, and we walk the yard with the customer before we leave.

You’ll do well here if you

  • Show up on time, ready to work, every time
  • Don’t mind weather, lifting and staying on your feet
  • Use equipment safely, and say so when you haven’t used something before
  • Respect the customer’s property: no shortcuts through flower beds
  • Can get yourself to jobs between DeKalb and St. Charles

No experience? That’s fine if you’re dependable and willing to learn. We’ll show you how we do every job.

Where you’ll work

Homes in DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Lily Lake, Campton Hills, Geneva, St. Charles and nearby communities.

Requirements

Age 18 or older and applicable work authorization. Veterans are encouraged to apply. All qualified applicants are welcome.

How to apply

The application takes about 5 minutes: contact information, the work you’ve done or want to learn, and your availability. You can describe your experience instead of uploading a resume; an optional PDF resume may be up to 1 MB. No account needed. Hours depend on the season and customer demand; we’ll be straight with you about what’s available. Please keep ID documents and other sensitive records out of your application. Need an accommodation?
