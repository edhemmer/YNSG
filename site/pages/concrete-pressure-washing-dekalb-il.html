Surfaces and water supply

We review surface condition, drainage and wash-water handling before agreeing to pressure wash. The customer supplies accessible outdoor water.

Is your concrete suitable for pressure washing?

Concrete age, existing cracks, loose surfaces, drainage and nearby landscaping can affect whether washing is appropriate. Describe these conditions and provide a photo if available. We review water access and runoff before accepting a driveway, walkway or patio project; not every surface is suitable.

What we do not wash

No roof, siding, elevated-area or delicate-surface pressure washing. We confirm whether a concrete job is suitable before agreeing to timing or pricing.

Planning driveway or patio pressure washing

Describe the concrete surface, approximate area, visible damage and nearby drainage. Tell us where accessible outdoor water is available. We review the surface condition and wash-water handling before deciding whether the job is suitable.

Can you wash siding or a roof during the same visit?

This service covers ground-level residential concrete driveways, walkways and patios. Roofs, siding, elevated areas and delicate surfaces are outside the washing scope. Concrete washing has separate pricing, confirmed after review.
