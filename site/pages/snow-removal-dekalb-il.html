A few helpful answers

What equipment and areas does this cover?

We use hand shovels or a walk-behind snow blower for residential driveways, sidewalks and entries. Road or commercial-lot plowing, salt and deicer application are outside this service.

How are timing and pricing arranged?

We review weather, access and availability before accepting a visit. This is not a guaranteed same-day emergency service. See snow-clearing pricing; we’ll confirm the work and timing with you.
