Planning driveway and sidewalk snow clearing

Describe the driveway, sidewalk and entrance areas you want cleared, including approximate size and any access restrictions. Tell us which path matters most. We review the request against weather, equipment and availability before confirming a visit.

Is a snow removal request a confirmed visit?

No. Sending a request lets us review the work and discuss timing; it does not reserve an emergency response or guarantee a clear surface. We confirm scope and snow-clearing pricing before accepting the job. Salt and deicer application are outside this service.
