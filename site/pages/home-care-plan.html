What’s included

  • Four seasonal visits, two hours each: spring cleanup, summer upkeep, fall leaves and winter prep, or the small home jobs on your list.
  • We schedule them for you. We reach out before each season with a time. You just say yes or pick another.
  • Priority booking for anything else you need between visits.
  • 10% off extra hours beyond your plan visits.
  • A free safety check every visit: smoke and CO detector test buttons and a quick look at the dryer vent.
  • A visit report after each visit, with what we did, before-and-after photos and anything we noticed.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your home with a request and mention the Home Care Plan.
  2. We walk through your list together and plan the four visits around your seasons.
  3. We show up as scheduled, do the work, and send your visit report.

Plan visits follow our usual rules: an agreed scope for each visit, no trip charges, and nothing beyond the plan without your approval. Materials, parts and disposal are separate at documented cost. Plan terms are confirmed in writing before you start.
