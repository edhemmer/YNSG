Where we work

We serve homes in DeKalb, Sycamore and Cortland, Illinois. Choose your city on the request form and tell us what you need. We’ll review the location, work and timing before confirming a visit.

What we can take off your list

Furniture assembly and small home jobs, lawn mowing and leaf management, hand weeding, planting small bushes and flowers, mulch pickup and spreading, residential snow clearing, and ground-level concrete pressure washing. You can put jobs from more than one category in a single request.

For concrete washing, please provide accessible outdoor water. For mulch and larger purchases, pay the supplier directly before an arranged pickup. We don’t apply herbicides, salt or deicer. See our full services and limits before making your list.

Appointment hours

Our usual appointment starts are Monday through Friday, from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., Central Time. Hourly visits have a two-hour minimum; a 3 p.m. start may finish at 5 p.m. Available times and the work are reviewed before we confirm your appointment.

Clear rates before we begin

Standard hourly labor is $60/hour. The $45/hour Community Rate is available for seniors 70+, veterans, single moms and folks with disabilities. A two-hour minimum applies to hourly visits. Lawn care, snow clearing and concrete washing have separate pricing. See pricing details.

Ready to arrange a visit?

No account is needed to submit a request. Choose your services and share your name, email, phone and service address. If you’d rather talk first, call or text 770-630-2094.
