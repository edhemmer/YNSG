Services available in this area

We review residential lawn mowing and trimming, leaf management, mulch and garden-bed work, small driveway and walkway snow clearing, ground-level concrete pressure washing, and small home jobs and furniture assembly. See the full service descriptions and limits.

Appointment hours

Our usual appointment starts are Monday through Friday, from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., Central Time. Hourly visits have a two-hour minimum; a 3 p.m. start may finish at 5 p.m. Available times and the work are reviewed before we confirm your appointment.
