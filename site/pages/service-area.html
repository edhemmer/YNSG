Where we work

Our home base is the 60115 area in DeKalb. Our expanded coverage reaches east toward St. Charles, including DeKalb, Sycamore, Cortland, Maple Park, Elburn, Kaneville, Burlington, Lily Lake, Virgil, Campton Hills, Geneva and St. Charles, as well as nearby communities around that corridor.

For planning, we use a broad service circle centered on 60115, with eastern St. Charles as its approximate outer reach. We don’t require customers to calculate a distance or fit a rigid radius. All genuine service requests can be submitted; we review travel, project scope and availability before agreeing to the work.

Request a time that works for you

You can choose a preferred appointment time when one is offered, or skip the calendar and ask us to arrange a time. The request form stays open even when no calendar slots appear. We may suggest nearby appointment days to group neighborhood jobs and save unnecessary travel, but no day is restricted to a particular town. A request is not a confirmed appointment; we’ll contact you to agree on timing, pricing and scope.

Home, yard and seasonal services

We offer lawn mowing and trimming, leaf management, mulch pickup and spreading, garden-bed care, residential snow clearing, concrete pressure washing, household cleanouts, small home jobs, and seasonal farm hand assistance. See the full service descriptions and safety limits.

Pricing and travel

We'll explain the applicable rates, minimums and any travel or disposal charges before confirming the job. No added travel fee is automatically imposed by the request form. View our pricing and Community Rate.
