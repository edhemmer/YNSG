## Facebook rules to follow (so the post isn't flagged)

- ✅ OK: "Feel free to share" or "pass this along." Sharing can be encouraged, but it can never count as an entry.
- ❌ Don't say "like, share or tag a friend to enter," and don't give extra entries for sharing or tagging.
- ✅ The only way to enter is the website form, which also captures the name, address and email for the marketing list.
- ✅ Keep the "not sponsored by Facebook/Meta" line on every post.
- ✅ Announce the winner only with their permission (first name and town only).
