# Service Saturday: Nextdoor posts

**Post from the Your Neighborhood Service Guy Business Page, not your personal profile.** Nextdoor
removes business promotion posted from personal accounts. Business Pages get about two posts a
month in the neighborhood feed, so use the main post now and the reminder around December 3.

Attach one real job photo (raking leaves or the basement shelving). Nextdoor readers respond to
real local faces, not graphics.

---

## Nextdoor tips

- Reply to every comment. Engagement is what keeps the post in neighbors' feeds.
- Entry is by the website form only (it collects name, address and email for your list). Don't run entries through comments.
- After the drawing, post a thank-you and the winner's first name and town only, and only with their permission.
