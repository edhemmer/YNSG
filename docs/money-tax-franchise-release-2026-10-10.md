## Implemented

- Money links to a tenant-scoped Tax workspace. Reviewed, versioned rules carry jurisdictions, service scope, address-sourcing evidence, effective dates, source links and rounding method. No default sales-tax percentage is assumed.
- Labor-only invoice review can apply a rule to all work and issue the invoice in one atomic owner action. Changes to work, tax setup, service address or business date invalidate stale tax assessments. Customer acceptance of the tax-inclusive total must be recorded. Materials and mixed taxable/exempt work are explicitly unsupported by this whole-labor calculation and remain review-required.
- Issued invoice snapshots preserve customer/address, approved work, explicit fixed amounts or billed minutes × rate, subtotal, each tax component, tax total and invoice total. Sales tax posts to tax payable, rather than labor revenue. PDF, customer view and invoice email use the same document amounts. Legacy invoices are not rewritten or given invented rates.
- Confirmed cash and Zelle payments can partially pay one invoice. Multiple receipts can cover an invoice; overpayments and duplicate commands are rejected. Zelle is money received through the owner's bank, not a fabricated payment gateway.
- Stripe adapter is card-only, USD, live-mode, with server-selected tenant merchants and a unique settlement-account mapping. It verifies merchant capability before new checkout, uses a stable database order/provider idempotency key, verifies signed raw-body webhooks, retrieves provider objects and records exact balance-transaction gross/fees/net. Checkout redirects do not mark an invoice paid. Prepared/open card checkouts block conflicting cash/Zelle entries. Known rejected creation releases the preparation; uncertain creation is retried with the same provider key. Canceling a checkout requires provider confirmation.
- Stripe successful refunds produce immutable negative payment corrections and balanced ledger entries. Webhook/provider replay is deduplicated. A card dispute produces a visible review alert; dispute chargebacks and bank payouts require reconciliation in Stripe and are not represented as confirmed bank cash. Customer surcharges are disabled; no estimated fee is added to customer charges.
- Owner/bookkeeper can record a customer-agreed due date and archive an invoice with an audited reason. Archiving preserves issued charges, balance and customer access. Archive/due/pending-card labels appear in Money. This does not delete accounting records or automatically email changed due terms.
- Federal planning uses the owner's completed 1040-ES annual payment amount, including applicable income/self-employment/other taxes, withholding and credits. It is not inferred from CRM receipts. Illinois includes a limited 2026 worksheet helper using net Illinois income after exemptions, surcharges, withholding and credits. Verified 2026 calendar-year individual installment dates are April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15, 2027; Illinois installment amounts follow whole-dollar worksheet rounding. Other entity calendars, other state calendars and 2027 schedules need reviewed authority dates.
- Tax deadlines, actual authority payments and corrections are append-only audited records. Opening a payment URL does not pay tax or file a return. CSV exports distinguish invoice totals from component rows and retain rule sources, exact integer cents, payment references and reversals. Spreadsheet formula injection is neutralized. This is an accountant mapping export, not a claim of universal QuickBooks/TurboTax import compatibility.
- Public `/franchise/` page and footer link: modern cloud technology and controlled AI, marketing/search/social/print support, personalized website, yard signs, two shirts (white/black/mixed), and proposed 15-mile-radius protection measured from the approved operating location within the owner's ZIP. No pricing or earnings promises. Final availability/support/territory is subject to disclosures and signed agreements. Call/text Ed paths work without a service-request form.
