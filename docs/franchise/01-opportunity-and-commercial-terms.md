# Your Neighborhood Service Guy Franchise Information

October 9 2026

This information describes the proposed Your Neighborhood Service Guy franchise package for prospective local owners. The program combines a consistent home and yard service brand with a centrally managed website and CRM. The owner supplies the qualified labor, local relationships and day-to-day business management. Required disclosures and the signed franchise agreement determine the parties' rights and obligations.

## The local business

Our philosophy is simple: We're Here to Help. Owners offer respectful, dependable service for neighbors who need smaller home and yard tasks completed. Seniors, veterans, single moms and folks with disabilities are part of the community we serve. Owners must communicate clearly, respect customer independence and avoid describing customers as helpless or making assumptions about their abilities.

The franchisee operates an independent business under a limited license to the Your Neighborhood Service Guy name and approved graphics. The franchisor retains ownership and control of the business brand, marks, website platform, domains, technology and central standards. This does not give the franchisor ownership of the franchisee's legal entity or unrestricted control of its employees, bank account or lawful service decisions.

## Commercial terms

The proposed initial franchise fee is $25,000. The proposed recurring technology and basic marketing support fee is $650 per month. The signed fee schedule must identify the payment date, commencement date, any applicable tax, approved payment method and the refund and cancellation terms before any payment is requested. These amounts do not establish total startup costs or required working capital.

No separate percentage royalty, renewal fee, transfer fee, mandatory advertising contribution or franchise financing arrangement is stated in this package. Such a charge cannot be assumed to be zero or collected unless it is expressly disclosed and agreed before the applicable transaction. The owner bears the expenses identified below and any separately approved purchases. Earlier proposed franchise and monthly fee amounts are superseded for this package.

## Included support

The proposed package includes a centrally managed branded local website, access to the contracted CRM modules, a licensed graphic package, initial yard signs and two work shirts. The owner may select two white shirts, two black shirts or one of each. Size and fit selections, yard sign quantity and specifications, delivery timing and shipping treatment must be stated in the supplied-items schedule. No tool or equipment purchase is mandatory merely because it appears in an example list.

Basic marketing support consists of initial local website SEO configuration and assistance with approved social media content and local print advertising designs. It includes brand-consistent materials and guidance within the written support scope. Specific quantities, cadence and response commitments are stated in the support schedule. Search rankings, leads, appointments, revenue and marketing results are not guaranteed.

## What personalized website means

A personalized website is a Your Neighborhood Service Guy website configured to display the local owner's name, business contact details, approved service area and approved services that the owner chooses to offer. It uses the system's shared brand, design, website terms and supported request workflow. The owner's separate legal business name appears where required, including seller information and invoices.

Personalization does not transfer the Your Neighborhood Service Guy business name, marks, logos, website source code, domain registration, hosting account, platform administration or system ownership. Owners receive role-based access to their licensed workspace. They may update supported local information and select lawful, insured services within brand standards. Custom site designs, code changes, additional domains and unsupported integrations require a separately approved scope.

## Costs the owner pays

The owner pays insurance, licenses, permits, labor, payroll, vehicles, fuel, tools, equipment, materials, disposal, bank charges, taxes and working capital. Customers pay for materials or reimbursements only as expressly agreed in the customer scope. Card processing charges and third-party fees must be disclosed in the applicable schedule; no unverified surcharge is added to a customer invoice.

All marketing outside the described included support is the owner's responsibility. Paid social or search advertising, boosted posts, local-services advertising, direct mail, additional printing, distribution, sponsorships, purchased leads, agencies and custom campaigns require the owner's approval and funding. Basic assistance does not include an advertising spend allowance. Additional approved work shirts are $40 each before any separately disclosed applicable tax or shipping.

## Territory protection

The proposed protected area is a 15-mile straight-line radius from the owner's verified and approved operating location within the business ZIP code. The signed territory exhibit records the street address, latitude, longitude, boundary map and effective date. A ZIP code alone is not a precise territory boundary.

During the applicable agreement term, the franchisor will not authorize another Your Neighborhood Service Guy franchise operating location inside that protected area, subject to the final territory grant and applicable law. Customer travel distance, online visibility, every lead and every customer are not automatically exclusive. Cross-boundary work, adjacent territory overlap, central inquiries and relocation require the written allocation policy. The operating location cannot be moved to expand or alter protection without approval and an updated signed exhibit.

## Owner readiness

Before launch, the owner must establish its legal business and tax registrations, confirm licensing and service limitations, obtain the required insurance, approve its services and prices, provide accurate website information, connect its business calendar and communications account, verify its settlement account, and complete the documented customer workflow acceptance run. The operator supplies the technology deployment; the owner should not need to manage hosting, database keys or software builds.

The proposed minimum insurance requirements are commercial general liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, automobile liability of $500,000 combined single limit, and workers' compensation when required by law and the labor arrangement. Endorsements, additional insured treatment and waivers of subrogation are detailed in the agreement insurance exhibit.

## Obtaining formal disclosures

The commercial and operating materials do not replace a Franchise Disclosure Document. The Federal Trade Commission requires a current disclosure document with 23 items and generally at least 14 calendar days before a binding agreement or payment. State registration, advertising and disclosure requirements also apply where required. The franchisor must complete those requirements before proceeding with an offer or sale in a regulated jurisdiction.
