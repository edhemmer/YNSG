# Your Neighborhood Service Guy Platform Operations and Acceptance

October 10 2026

This internal operating specification assigns platform and owner responsibilities and defines evidence required before a new owner's website and CRM launch. It is not prospect marketing material. A completed source build or configured provider is not a substitute for demonstrated delivery through the production custom domains.

## Responsibility boundaries

The platform operator controls organization provisioning, deployment, domain routing, canonical branding, server secrets, database authorization, worker execution, monitoring and release recovery. The local owner controls accurate contact/seller data, lawful service selection, availability, scope and price approval, business account consent, customer commitments, actual work and payment confirmation. Professional legal, tax and insurance decisions require the appropriate qualified review.

The retained franchise system is the Your Neighborhood Service Guy name, marks, graphic package, site platform, domains and central standards. Local personalization is owner name/contact/service area and approved service choices. Separate organization records and role checks are required for requests, customer documents, Google tokens, taxes and Stripe settlement. A platform subscription administrator must not gain customer-data access merely to administer subscriptions.

## New owner provisioning

Verify executed lawful franchise documentation, required insurance and an approved territory point/map before enabling a new licensed business. Create one organization and invite only the verified owner identity. Provision applicable module entitlements, approved canonical brand assets and a local website routing record. Bind each website's intake server to its actual organization; do not accept a client-selected organization as an authorization boundary.

The owner should supply business choices through ordinary settings rather than configuring infrastructure. A repeat provisioning command must not create a second organization, domain, calendar or merchant mapping. Configuration versions and audit receipts preserve changes. The provisioning implementation must be verified with two independent organizations before claiming automated franchise deployment.

## Public website and service synchronization

Publish the approved local owner name, contact, seller disclosures, service area and offered services. The public service list, form validation and CRM catalog must use the same approved version. A held or retired service must not be bookable from stale website content. Keep historical records intact. Explicitly show the state in which a newer configuration has not yet reached the public site.

Verify canonical URLs, sitemap, contact links, mobile actions, required request fields, spam defenses and server-side duplicate protection. Images use the approved logo and depict the actual offered services. The hero uses the agreed five-second fade interval, with appropriate reduced-motion handling; verify real auto-advance on a phone. Do not claim full accessibility from a static syntax check.

## Integrations and deterministic authority

Calendar and Gmail access use owner consent, protected tokens, the approved business sender and actual provider results. Maps uses restricted browser/server credentials and configured quota limits. Routes display verified locations and time-sensitive provider estimates. Google Ads or Local Services API enablement is not proof of account authorization or working automated marketing.

Stripe requires a live server-only key, correct tenant account mapping and the actual production signing secret. Use card-only checkout, a pinned API version, stable provider idempotency, raw-body signature verification and retrieved provider facts. Uncertain requests retain their reservation until reconciled. Refunds are immutable corrections; disputed card charges and bank payouts need explicit reconciliation. No generated agent output may mark funds paid, change tax liability or silently move a booked appointment.

## Communication acceptance

Exercise every applicable owner/customer template: intake, request review, confirmed time, decline, changed time, reminder, reschedule, delay, invoice/PDF, payment thank-you/review and connection test. Record the actual sender, recipient, subject, content and destination. Customer buttons must open the correct authorized task and custom domain. Private request IDs are not exposed as a customer security capability.

Separate queued, provider accepted, delivered/read where available, failed and uncertain results. Confirm real inbox and phone receipt independently. Do not resend a message with an unknown provider outcome automatically. Authentication and recovery emails are tested through the actual auth provider in addition to business Gmail templates. Sample suites must remain labeled SAMPLE and must not create real appointments or accounting receipts.

## Financial acceptance

For each approved service, verify time times rate or fixed amount, waiver, customer/address, tax jurisdiction/rule, subtotal, components, total, due terms and identical PDF/email/customer view. Tax collected posts to a liability. Test partial cash, Zelle and card splits, pending-card conflicts, signed webhook replay, actual processor fees, refund balances and retained archived records. No synthetic fixture counts as a bank receipt.

Income tax planning uses reviewed owner worksheets and verified jurisdiction/year calendars. Record actual authority payments separately from planned installments. Export immutable invoices, receipts, tax components, corrections and source evidence. Confirm the accountant's destination mapping rather than claiming universal QuickBooks or tax software compatibility.

## Security and continuity

Check tenant denial, current membership, individual sessions, origin protections, rate limits, RLS and least-privilege RPCs. Secrets never enter browser variables or prospect documents. Owner brand edits are blocked at the server and database boundary. Use supported password recovery, compromised-password protection where the provider plan permits and revocation of departed users.

Establish supported backup schedules, retention, restore verification, incident contact, monitoring and alert destinations before contractual uptime or recovery commitments. Run and record an actual restore exercise in an isolated environment. An installed extension, cron success or green build does not prove a business workflow is healthy. Document a safe manual continuity procedure and reconcile duplicate risk when service returns.

## Launch record

Record organization and deployment version; custom domains; owner acceptance date; legal/insurance/territory evidence; actual website lead; appointment/provider event; communication attempts and receipts; route demonstration; issued invoice; actual payment/provider event; settlement reconciliation; customer access; tax setup; mobile/keyboard/accessibility checks; failure recovery; and authorized launch approval. Protect customer details in the evidence store.

If a required check is unavailable or failed, report the exact affected function and next action. Do not display Production ready or a blanket Passed status. A new owner may not be launched on an unverified merchant, unapproved service or guessed territory. Native iOS starts only after the web release meets the documented acceptance and stability criteria.
