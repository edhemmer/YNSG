# Your Neighborhood Service Guy Owner Operations Manual

October 9 2026

This manual explains the local owner's service workflow, brand responsibilities and marketing boundaries. It supports the signed franchise agreement and completed support schedule. It cannot alter the agreed fees, territory or statutory rights. The operating goal is a clear customer experience with accurate records and a short, understandable owner workflow.

## Owner setup

Supply your legal seller name, owner name, business phone/email, service area, operating hours, insurance, lawful service choices, rates and approved customer terms. The platform operator provisions the website, organization and authorized owner account. Owners manage their business settings and consent to their own calendar, email and payment connections. Hosting, database administration and server keys remain with the platform operator.

The local website retains the Your Neighborhood Service Guy name and official logo. It shows the owner's name and contact details, approved service area and selected approved services. Review the customer-facing website before launch, including mobile text, buttons, contact links, services, prices, terms and seller identity. A saved setting is not proof that a public website has updated; verify the deployed customer view.

## Service selection and safe scope

Choose services you can actually perform lawfully and competently with appropriate insurance and equipment. Approved categories may include lawn care, yard and garden tasks, residential snow clearing, concrete pressure washing and smaller home tasks. Describe actual included work and exclusions. Hold services that you cannot deliver. Preserve retired services in history rather than deleting past customer records.

Baseline exclusions include ladders, work above five feet, gutter cleaning, ceiling fans, herbicides, salt application and plowing roads or parking lots. A service requiring a license, permit or code work goes to an appropriately licensed contractor under a clearly documented arrangement. No graphic, generated text or customer request authorizes prohibited work. Remove any marketing image that conflicts with the actual service policy or contains a visibly inaccurate action.

## Incoming requests

The customer gives name, phone, email, service address and requested services. Multiple tasks may be included in one visit. Account creation is optional. A requested date/time is a preference until the owner confirms it. A request without a date remains a lead to schedule; it must not disappear because no appointment exists yet.

The owner summary should show customer, address, requested services, proposed date/time if any and current status. Approve a suitable proposed time; decline with an appropriate factual message; use Review for the details or a changed time. When choosing a time, verify the saved appointment appears on the selected day and the confirmation is queued. Do not tell a customer an appointment is confirmed from a button click whose result failed.

## Scheduling and routes

Use the business calendar, correct local timezone, configured hours, service duration and travel allowance. Check nearby appointments and address quality before changing a day. Existing capacity and revision rules protect against conflicting reservations. An override requires a documented reason and must not conceal a double booking.

Day routes show the selected date's actual appointments and verified locations. Traffic-aware provider estimates are time-sensitive and may change. The displayed route does not guarantee every detour, construction zone, signal or road restriction is known. Use current directions when traveling and stop safely before editing the CRM. A route suggestion must not silently move a customer appointment; obtain any required customer agreement and send the updated confirmation.

If running late, use the CRM's delay notice with a realistic revised arrival estimate. Review the recipient and result. A provider accepted message is different from a customer reading it. If delivery fails or timing is urgent, contact the customer using the approved alternative channel and record that action.

## Performing and completing work

Confirm the scope, pricing and materials arrangement before work begins. Customers supply water for pressure washing where stated. Disclose supplier pickup time when it is billable. Customers pay suppliers directly for mulch or larger items where required by the agreed policy. Do not add hidden charges or spend money beyond the customer's authorization.

Record actual work, time and changes. Customer-approved fixed prices and hourly calculations must remain distinguishable. A courtesy task has a recorded reason and zero charge. Complete the service record when finished; completion alone does not approve an invoice or prove payment. Report damage, unsafe conditions or scope conflicts promptly and stop the affected work when necessary.

## Invoices and payment

Review the customer identity and address, services, actual or billed time, hourly rate or fixed price, materials treatment, tax components, total and due terms. Approve the invoice only when the customer's agreement and applicable tax treatment are documented. Issued invoices preserve their approved amounts. Use the recorded correction or refund process rather than rewriting the original invoice.

Cash and Zelle are recorded only after actually received. Zelle is an external bank payment, not a CRM card gateway. Card payments use the configured Stripe checkout and verified signed provider events. Partial payments and mixed cash/card receipts may settle a single balance, but an open card checkout must be resolved before recording a conflicting manual payment. A return to the website does not establish that the card charge succeeded.

Actual processor fees are taken from the provider's balance transaction. No guessed percentage or fee is entered as an actual charge. Customer surcharges remain disabled unless the platform supports a separately reviewed lawful policy. Refunds, disputes and bank deposits require their own records and reconciliation. Funds pending with Stripe are not represented as deposited bank cash.

## Taxes and records

Maintain actual federal, state and local tax registrations and filing responsibilities. Sales tax and income tax serve different purposes. Tax collected from a customer is a liability to the authority, not service income. Review the tax treatment of the precise service and location rather than assuming every labor charge has the same state rate.

The Tax workspace tracks reviewed rules, deadlines, worksheet-based planned income tax installments and actual authority payments. The owner supplies its filing status, entity, other income, deductions, withholding and credits with its tax adviser. CRM receipts alone do not determine personal federal or state income tax. Payment links do not file a return or prove a tax payment. Verify each year and jurisdiction independently; do not carry a prior-year deadline into a new year without authority review.

Export records for the accountant and verify the receiving software's mapping. A CSV export is not a guarantee of direct compatibility with every accounting or tax product. Archive invoices to simplify the workspace while preserving original charges, balances and lawful customer access. Never delete an accounting record to hide an unpaid or disputed amount.

## Brand and graphics

Use the current approved name, logo, colors and supplied graphic package. Do not redraw the logo, stretch it, substitute unapproved colors or add a competing business name. Keep website and phone text readable at the intended print size. Review proofs before ordering shirts, signs or advertising. Use approved white/black artwork for the actual background.

Show authentic service actions and accurate tools, hands, furniture and scale. Do not present a generated image as an actual customer's testimonial or actual completed job. Obtain permission for identifiable customer photos and avoid disclosing medical conditions or home security information. Remove misleading licensing, insurance, price and guarantee claims.

## Included marketing and owner responsibility

The monthly support schedule defines basic local website SEO work, social media content assistance and local print design assistance. It states the actual quantities, revision limits, cadence and delivery process. Request support through the agreed channel, supply accurate local information and approve any public claim or owner expense.

The owner is responsible for marketing outside that described support. Advertising spend, boosting, purchased leads, direct mail, additional printing, distribution, sponsorships, agencies and custom campaigns are owner-funded unless a separate written inclusion applies. Approval of a design is not authorization to buy a media campaign. Record the budget, channel, dates and owner approval before any spend. Search ranking or lead volume is never promised.

## Customer respect and complaints

Speak plainly and respectfully. Use Request service appt or Request services as the action label. Keep the process easy for phone users and clearly distinguish clickable actions from informational text. Explain what happens next without burying the customer in account requirements or repeated approvals.

Record complaints factually, including scope, dates, customer concern and response. Do not deny a statutory remedy through an automated message. Escalate insurance incidents and legal disputes to the appropriate professional. Never pressure a customer for a review, publish private dispute details or offer an unauthorized assurance that the franchisor will pay a claim.

## Insurance and labor

Maintain required GL $1M/$2M, business auto $500K CSL and applicable WC. Review the actual insured operations, territories and endorsements with the insurance professional. Additional insured and waiver status require policy endorsements, not merely a certificate. Workers' compensation uses the appropriate waiver where available; it is not treated as a general liability AI policy.

Review employee and contractor classification before engaging labor. The W2/1099 label does not waive coverage requirements. Keep subcontractor licenses, insurance and scope records current. Report changes in operations, vehicles, employees, subcontractors or territory before assuming the policy covers them.

## Account access and outages

Use individual accounts, a unique password and the supported recovery process. Remove access when a worker leaves. Never share a server key or use another owner's account. Check the workspace's recorded delivery/connection status when a calendar or email action fails. Avoid repeated sends when the provider result is uncertain; review the saved attempt first.

During an outage, preserve the request and customer contact, use a documented phone/manual appointment process and reconcile the CRM when service resumes. Do not create duplicate leads, appointments or receipts to simulate recovery. The platform operator owns technical incident response; the owner owns accurate customer communication and its business decisions.

## Launch acceptance and continuing checks

Before advertising, demonstrate a real authorized request through the website, owner approval and calendar placement, customer/owner notifications, route access, work completion, customer-approved invoice, confirmed payment, receipt, customer document access and archived history. Verify on the owner's actual phone and in both inboxes. Synthetic samples illustrate templates; they do not prove those real actions occurred.

Review open requests, today's appointments, delivery exceptions, unpaid balances and disputed payments regularly. Confirm monthly fees and separately approved marketing charges against the contract. Native iOS development follows stabilized, demonstrated web acceptance; a home-screen shortcut does not establish a tested native app.
