## Grant of license

Subject to this agreement and applicable law, the franchisor grants the franchisee a limited, nontransferable license during the agreed term to operate one approved local home and yard service business under the Your Neighborhood Service Guy brand at the approved operating location. The license covers the approved graphics, local website and contracted CRM modules. All rights not expressly granted are retained by the franchisor or their respective owners.

The franchisee owns its separate legal business and remains responsible for service delivery, customer contracts, employment decisions, lawful pricing, permits, taxes, insurance, equipment and local expenses. Neither party may bind the other, incur debt in the other's name or represent that their separate legal entities are the same employer. Brand and technology control do not transfer the franchisee's business ownership to the franchisor.

## Fees and payment

The initial franchise fee is $25,000. The technology and basic marketing support fee is $650 per month. Exhibit A must specify when the initial fee becomes due, when monthly billing starts, the monthly due date, method of payment, treatment of a partial first or last month and any applicable tax. The initial fee is paid only after applicable disclosure and waiting requirements are satisfied.

Late charges, interest, collection charges, annual increases, renewal charges and transfer charges are not imposed unless expressly stated in the disclosed fee schedule and lawful. No franchisor may debit a bank account or card without the account holder's separate written authorization. A fee increase or reduction of contracted support during the term requires the contract mechanism expressly disclosed and agreed by the parties; the operations manual alone cannot change commercial fees.

## Supplied items and optional purchases

Exhibit B identifies initial yard signs, two approved work shirts and the graphic package. Shirts may be white, black or one of each. Sizes, garment model, delivery address, sign quantity, dimensions, proof approval and delivery timing are completed before execution. Tools, equipment and vehicles are selected and purchased by the owner unless an optional written purchase schedule is accepted. Any supplier restrictions, affiliate benefit or required purchasing arrangement must be disclosed as required. The franchisee must use safe equipment appropriate to its actual approved work; optional ownership does not excuse safety obligations.

## Protected operating location

Exhibit C grants the agreed location protection: no other Your Neighborhood Service Guy franchise operating location may be authorized inside a 15-mile straight-line radius from the franchisee's verified approved operating point during the protected term. The exhibit contains address, ZIP code, latitude, longitude, an authoritative boundary map and the effective date. Measurements use the stated geographic calculation and verified coordinates, not a road route or an arbitrary ZIP centroid.

Before any territory is granted, the franchisor checks existing locations and proposed boundaries, records any overlap and discloses adjacent-location and customer allocation rules. A second operating point less than 15 miles from the protected center is prohibited. Centers 15 to less than 30 miles apart may produce overlapping service areas; that overlap must be addressed before granting the adjacent territory. No promise of exclusive ownership of all customers, national accounts, advertising impressions or online inquiries is implied.

Relocation, satellite offices, additional units and cross-boundary marketing require written approval and any required amendment or disclosure. The franchisor cannot shrink the signed protected area through an informal map or later manual revision. Address confidentiality and publication of a home-based location must be handled separately from internal territory verification.

## Website personalization and retained rights

The personalized website displays the owner's name, contact details, approved service area and selected approved services within the Your Neighborhood Service Guy website. The franchisor retains the business brand and controlling interest in the licensed website, platform, domains, central graphics, source code, deployment administration and system standards. This retained interest concerns the licensed system; it does not create ownership of the franchisee entity or unrestricted access to its customer records.

Supported local fields may be updated through approved controls. The franchisee may choose which approved services it offers and may hold or retire a service while preserving historical records. The franchisee may not substitute a different business brand, change the official logo, redirect a system domain, copy the platform for an unrelated business, transfer credentials or install unsupported code. The separate seller's legal identity remains accurate on customer contracts and invoices.

## Services and regulated work

The franchisee offers only work it is qualified, insured, equipped and legally permitted to perform. Service selection is subject to documented scope and exclusions. Any work requiring a trade license, permit, inspection or code compliance is performed only by an appropriately licensed contractor whose applicable insurance and authorization have been verified. A referral is distinguished from work contracted or performed by the franchisee.

The baseline YNSG service policy excludes ladders and work above five feet, gutter cleaning, ceiling fans, herbicides, salt application and road or parking-lot plowing unless a later expressly approved lawful service policy applies. An owner cannot use a catalog checkbox to override a prohibition or license requirement. The website, customer scope and operating policy must agree before a service is published.

## Technology and controlled automation

The franchisor supplies the contracted technology access described in Exhibit D. Requests, calendars, routes, invoices and communications use recorded business rules and available provider integrations. AI may assist with drafting and organization within authorized boundaries. AI does not decide licensing, worker classification, tax treatment, promised customer arrival times or recorded payments from an unsupported prediction.

Customer financial records require actual issued documents and confirmed payment events. A successful checkout redirect, manual note or generated message does not establish receipt of card funds. Third-party outages, revoked consent, inaccurate addresses, provider limits and missing owner inputs may interrupt service. No absolute uptime, cybersecurity, response time or autonomous business result is guaranteed; an enforceable service level exists only when specifically completed in Exhibit D.

## Marketing scope and owner expenses

The $650 monthly fee includes the basic technology and marketing support described in Exhibit D. Basic marketing includes local website SEO setup and maintenance within the contracted scope, assistance with social media content and approved local print advertising designs. The parties complete actual quantities, cadence, channels and support response terms rather than relying on an undefined unlimited service promise.

Marketing outside that described support is the owner's responsibility. The owner pays approved media spend, boosted posts, search or local-services advertising, additional print production, distribution, sponsorships, purchased leads, agencies and custom campaigns. No spend may be incurred in the owner's name without its written approval. Neither party may invent testimonials, imply an unearned license, promise guaranteed results or make a financial performance representation outside a compliant disclosure.

## Insurance and endorsements

Before operations and continuously during the agreement, the franchisee maintains the insurance required by Exhibit E: commercial general liability at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate; business automobile liability at least $500,000 combined single limit for vehicles and exposures used in operations; and statutory workers' compensation where required. Employer's liability, hired/nonowned auto and any service-specific coverage are completed with the owner's licensed insurance professional according to actual exposures.

Franchisor and designated entities are additional insureds on GL and automobile policies to the extent the policy, endorsement and law permit. GL treatment must address ongoing and completed operations where applicable. Waivers of subrogation are required on GL, auto and WC where available and lawful. Workers' compensation ordinarily does not use additional insured status; WC requires the appropriate waiver endorsement and applicable coverage, not an invented AI endorsement. Any requested status not available on a policy must be disclosed and resolved before launch.

Certificates alone do not establish additional insured or waiver status. Copies of applicable endorsements, named-insured accuracy, covered operations, limits, effective dates and material exclusions are reviewed. Primary/noncontributory treatment and insurer notice of cancellation are required only to the extent agreed and actually supported. The owner promptly reports lapse, cancellation or a material change; a policy is not represented as covering an excluded operation.

## Employees and subcontractors

Approved subcontractors maintain required licenses and insurance, including WC where applicable, and sign scope and confidentiality obligations appropriate to their work.

The franchisee documents credentials and insurance before assigning work and does not permit an uninsured worker to perform a required insured exposure. No employment, joint-employer or independent-contractor conclusion is guaranteed by this agreement's terminology.

## Customer commitments and conduct

Cancellation, refund, workmanship and complaint terms must be lawful and included in the customer agreement. This franchise agreement does not impose a universal nonrefundable customer payment or remove a statutory remedy. Safety issues, complaints, damage and disputed charges are recorded promptly with factual evidence. No owner may promise work, warranties or outcomes beyond its ability and lawful scope.

## Default and proportionate remedies

Material default includes unlawful service, material misuse of the marks, required insurance lapse, unauthorized customer-data disclosure, fraudulent records, or failure to pay an agreed lawful charge. The franchisor gives written notice identifying the facts, required correction and applicable cure period. Cure periods, immediate-termination grounds and remedies are completed in Exhibit G and applicable state addenda. Mandatory franchise relationship protections prevail over inconsistent language.

Temporary suspension may be limited to the affected unsafe service or compromised account while an actual urgent risk is reviewed. Suspension is not a device to bypass termination protections. Customer records, outstanding balances and already promised service are handled through a documented continuity plan. The franchisee has a documented process to report a disputed default and evidence of correction.

## Cancellation and termination

Issued invoices and lawful customer record access are preserved during the agreed transition and retention period. Customer funds are never forfeited to the franchisor merely because a franchise ends. Export timing, open-job communications and domain handling must be completed before execution.

The indemnity excludes loss caused by the franchisor's own negligence, willful misconduct or legal violation to the extent required by law. The franchisor remains responsible for claims arising from its own conduct.

Claim notice, defense control, cooperation, insurance coordination and settlement consent are completed in Exhibit G.
