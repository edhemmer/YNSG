# Your Neighborhood Service Guy Franchise Disclosure Preparation

October 9 2026

This preparation document identifies the information and legal work required to complete a Franchise Disclosure Document and lawful franchise sales process. It is not a completed FDD, registration approval or authority to sell. The actual franchisor entity, financial statements, business history, trademark authority and applicable state filings must be established from records rather than filled with invented facts.

## Federal disclosure timing

Under 16 CFR 436.2, a prospective franchisee generally must receive the current FDD at least 14 calendar days before signing a binding agreement or paying the franchisor or an affiliate in connection with the sale. If the franchisor unilaterally and materially changes an attached agreement, a separate seven-calendar-day revised-agreement period applies; changes initiated by the prospective franchisee in negotiation do not trigger that specific period. Counsel must assess exemptions and any longer or different state requirements.

Record the exact FDD version, complete delivered documents, recipient, delivery method, date/time and earliest permitted execution/payment date. Preserve the receipt without backdating it. A promotional page, unsigned agreement form or website disclaimer does not replace disclosure or cure an unlawful offer.

## State registration and advertising

Illinois registers and monitors franchisors through the Attorney General's Franchise Bureau. Establish whether an effective registration or a valid exemption is required before offering or selling into Illinois. Review advertising, filing, renewal and relationship rules separately. Other states may require registration, notice filings, broker/seller requirements or state-specific contract changes. Review where the prospect resides, where the outlet operates and where the offer is made.

Do not publish an availability claim for an unapproved state or collect a reservation fee as a workaround. The public franchise information page must be reviewed against the actual legal sales status; the phrase Not an offer alone is not a substitute for that review. Fees stay off the public page under the current business policy, while lawful FDD and private fee disclosures remain complete.

## FDD Items 1 through 4

Item 1 identifies the franchisor, parents, predecessors, affiliates, entity facts, addresses, business description and relevant regulation. Obtain verified formation records and the proposed entity's actual operations. Do not assume InLight AI LLC is the franchisor without an express entity decision and authority review.

Item 2 gives the required business experience of specified executives and franchise management. Item 3 covers required litigation disclosures, including franchise, regulatory and material actions under the rule. Item 4 covers required bankruptcy history. Obtain actual signed questionnaires and supporting checks; an absence of supplied facts is not a verified None.

## FDD Items 5 through 7

Item 5 states initial fees, here the proposed $25,000 franchise fee, payment timing and refund conditions. Item 6 states other fees, including the proposed $650 monthly technology and basic marketing support fee, any required vendor charges and other approved charges. Describe additional $40 shirts and separately disclosed tax/shipping in the appropriate place. No royalty, renewal or transfer fee should be invented.

Item 7 provides the required estimated initial investment ranges with actual bases: fees, supplies, insurance, licenses, equipment, vehicles, training costs, deposits, working capital and other applicable categories. Use supplier evidence and reasonable, disclosed methodology. The $25,000 initial fee is not the total investment. Owner choice of tools does not justify omitting reasonably necessary operating equipment costs.

## FDD Items 8 through 11

Item 8 discloses supplier restrictions, required purchases and applicable franchisor/affiliate benefits. Identify ownership of graphic, shirt, sign, hosting and payment arrangements and any rebates or financial interest. Item 9 summarizes franchisee obligations with accurate agreement/manual references. Item 10 describes financing actually offered; do not imply available financing from the absence of a program.

Item 11 describes franchisor assistance, advertising, computer systems and training. Define personalized website fields, retained brand/platform rights, contracted CRM modules and owner-managed service choices. Specify included SEO, social content and print design quantities, cadence, limits and owner-paid advertising. Identify support hours, provider/usage expenses, upgrade obligations, data access and training requirements. Disclose actual capability and legal commitments, not a promise that AI runs every business task.

## FDD Items 12 through 16

Item 12 describes territory and reserved rights. State the proposed 15-mile straight-line operating-location protection and signed point/map. Address overlap, online inquiries, cross-boundary work, relocation and the actual extent of protection; do not describe limited location protection as unrestricted customer exclusivity.

Item 13 identifies trademarks and actual registration/status and rights. Item 14 identifies relevant patents, copyrights and proprietary information. Item 15 describes the owner's required participation and any manager rules. Item 16 describes restrictions on goods or services offered. Distinguish the owner's elective service catalog from centrally prohibited, unlicensed or uninsured work.

## FDD Items 17 through 20

Item 17 summarizes renewal, termination, transfer and dispute provisions with precise contract references and state limitations. Complete the initial term, cure periods, refund policy, exit, transfer, succession and venue decisions before preparing this table. Item 18 discloses any public figure arrangements actually present.

Item 19 covers financial performance representations. No revenue, profit, lead, return or break-even claim is authorized by this package. If a lawful Item 19 is developed, it requires substantiation and required disclosures before the same representation is used in a page, email, conversation or AI-generated sales message. A disclaimer cannot make an unsupported earnings claim acceptable.

Item 20 requires outlet and franchisee information and the applicable historical tables and contacts. Establish actual outlet counts, transfers, closures, projections and franchisee contacts; do not manufacture a successful network from a planned program.

## FDD Items 21 through 23

Item 21 includes financial statements required by the rule, subject to any applicable permitted phase-in and state requirements. Obtain an accountant's determination of the actual audited statement requirements and applicable state conditions. No fabricated statements, solvency claim or readiness certificate may be substituted.

Item 22 attaches all proposed contracts, including the franchise agreement, completed material schedules, guaranty if any and required ancillary agreements. Item 23 includes the required receipt pages. Attach correct versions and preserve delivery evidence. The FDD cover, risk factors, table of contents and state addenda must also satisfy the applicable format rules.

## Documents required from the franchisor

Collect verified entity/ownership records and licensing authority; management history questionnaires; litigation/bankruptcy records; trademark and copyright rights; financial statements and accountant support; actual supplier and provider contracts; substantiated investment ranges; outlet histories; detailed support/marketing schedule; territory policy and maps; agreement terms and state addenda; privacy and incident procedures; and required seller, registration or exemption filings.

Commercial choices still to complete include contract duration, renewal, commencement and payment dates, initial-fee refund/cancellation policy, support quantities and response commitments, initial sign quantity, shipping, required employer's liability limits, export/retention terms, cure/exit periods and approved dispute terms. The known fee amounts and protection model do not resolve those fields.

## Sales process controls

Maintain a jurisdiction register showing where information, offers and sales are permitted and the current registration/exemption evidence. Version every prospect packet. Deliver the correct current FDD and contracts, record the receipt and calculate the waiting dates. Check seller authority and training. Release agreements and fee collection only after all required disclosure, state and commercial fields are complete.

Do not use an AI agent to answer unresolved legal or earnings questions with an invented assurance. Escalate to the designated authorized person and record the answer and source. Material updates require legal review and any required refreshed disclosure. Preserve records of actual representations and prospect complaints.
