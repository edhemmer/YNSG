## Shared UI contract

Every family above inherits: visible page title and one primary task, consistent terminology, labeled 44px touch controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, no horizontal overflow at 320/375/390/430/768/1366px, light/dark where supported, and enlarged text. Secondary information is disclosed progressively without losing originals. Loading, empty, failed, denied and unknown outcomes are distinct. Busy consequential actions cannot duplicate; success is shown only from verified receipt. Entry safety guards leave/back/company changes. No new competing controls or duplicate authoritative business logic.
