# Owner action-panel redesign — 1 November 2026

This candidate builds on PR 229's compact action-first job layout. The remaining usability gap was separation between the named next action and its form: approval, scheduling, invoice and payment tools still appeared after progress/help content, and three jump links added another step.

The active estimate/approval form, scheduling review, work review, timer and billing tools now live inside the main Current field action panel. Redundant links to the approval, invoice and payment form are removed; the actual form is already present. At invoice time, the work/charge review comes before task-time reference tools. Customer instructions, unknown-result recovery and pending replacement-time notices remain above the primary action. Full saved scope, progress, planning, files and history remain available. The 120-character agreed-scope preview introduced by the current baseline is preserved.

Planning buttons name their destination: review appointment, work/price, invoice or payments, or open the request. Agreement preparation has a separate local read-only action kind from missing-address review, so the address check still opens the full request and estimate preparation can return directly to its form. No command, API, schema, money, approval evidence, quote version, permission or retry semantics change. Original task-timer instances remain keyed to the job; moving the layout does not add another timer or reset drafts.

Acceptance: open the job and act on the visible form; record real approval explicitly; start/pause/resume actual work deliberately; review actual work and invoice before issuing; record only received funds. Declined/canceled/invalid saved states retain their guards. No customer messages or production test records are created. Existing brand, service catalog (including Farm hand day), pricing and free-only budget remain unchanged.

Local evidence before release: 71 targeted workflow/preparation/sample tests, 36 owner interaction/layout checks and 56 complete request-to-receipt/browser stages passed with synthetic fixtures. The browser journey explicitly checks that estimate, customer agreement, invoice issue and payment entry are within Current field action. Normal and enlarged text, phone/tablet/desktop, light/dark and full-source permission/recovery coverage remain required in exact-head CI. Production builds, full release/native PostgreSQL CI, tested-tree merge and read-only deployment checks must pass before publication. Actual owner/device acceptance remains separate under WEB-10; this does not close whole-product gates.
