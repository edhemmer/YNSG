# 1. Business and goals

## The business

Your Neighborhood Service Guy, LLC is a one-owner home and yard service business serving DeKalb to St. Charles, Illinois. Owner and operator: Ed Hemmer. Public phone 815-680-1775, business email info@yourneighborhoodserviceguy.com. Tagline "We're Here to Help."

Ed started the business because finding conventional work has been hard. It has to earn trust quickly with neighbors, so the tone is warm, honest and local, never corporate.

## Services (what we sell)

- **Hourly home and yard labor.** Standard rate $70 per person per hour (first-person two-hour minimum $140). Community Rate $45 per person per hour (minimum $90) for seniors 70+, veterans, single parents and people with disabilities. Additional people bill actual worked time with no extra minimum.
- Typical jobs: furniture assembly, shelving, door hardware, smart-lock installation, small drywall patches, household product setup, moving manageable items, grab bars and accessibility help, garden-bed cleanup, **weed pulling**, small bush trimming, planting, mulch spreading and pickup, leaf management, garage or storage cleanouts.
- **Per-job services:** lawn mowing (standard lots up to one-third acre start at $50), snow clearing (hand shovel or walk-behind blower, no salt or deicer), concrete washing (ground-level, customer supplies accessible outdoor water).
- **Farm hand services**: quoted separately, four-hour minimum.
- Pricing is estimate plus a not-to-exceed cap (usually about 25% above the estimate). The customer pays for time worked, subject to the minimum, and we stop and ask before passing the cap. Materials, parts and disposal are separate at documented actual cost.

The authoritative public wording is [`site/pages/pricing.html`](../../site/pages/pricing.html). Do not change prices or service boundaries without the owner.

## Product mandate

Build a production-ready, plain-words job management and billing system comparable to Jobber (inspired, not copied), with an ultra-premium look and feel, matched to the YNSG brand, usable by one owner from a phone in the field. Smarter and easier than generic tools: the app should tell the owner what needs attention today.

## Principles that shape every decision

1. **Free first.** No paid services without the owner's approval and a stated price.
2. **Honest and safe.** Never promise a quote, a booking or a price the system has not confirmed. A held time is a hold, not a confirmation.
3. **Plain language.** Labels say what they do. No jargon in customer or owner screens.
4. **Mobile first.** The owner works from a phone.
5. **Evidence is retained.** Submissions and issued invoices are records. Corrections are explicit, audited commands.
6. **Small business reality.** One owner, limited time, so automate follow-up but keep the human voice.
